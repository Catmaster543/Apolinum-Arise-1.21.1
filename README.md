# What's this mod about?
This is a Minecraft mod made for neoforge 1.21.1, that is mainly themed around an unknown infection.

## Features

### Awakening structure and block
Generating in the wild, you might find a abondoned, church lookin structure.
If you decide to venture down into its basement and complete the puzzles hidden within you'll find the awakening block. By interacting with the awakening block in any-way you'll begin the infection and permanently get rid of all awakening blocks in that world.

### Blood moons
Upon triggering the awakening block blood moons will now get chance to take place. Just like in many other games, during a blood moon the sky turns deep red and all enemy mobs become stronger. For each night that a blood moon does *not* happen, the higher chance for it is to happen the next night. To make your life just a little bit more miserable - as a helthy player you'll get weaker, depending on the moon phase (the fuller the moon, the stronger the enemies and weaker you).
